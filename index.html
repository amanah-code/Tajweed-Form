Your Qur'an journey deserves personal attention.

Many of us learned how to read the Qur'an, but never formally learned Tajweed.

We learned the letters.
We learned the words.
We learned how to read.

But perhaps, somewhere along the way, we stopped learning.

We were taught how to read Qur'an, but not always how to recite it with Tajweed.

And that doesn't mean it's too late.

Why Tajweed?

Allah says:

"And recite the Qur'an with measured recitation."Surah Al-Muzzammil 73:4

The Qur'an is the Word of Allah.

Every letter we recite is an act of worship. Learning Tajweed is about care, precision, and respect for the words of Allah.

If the Qur'an is an Amanah, shouldn't we strive to recite it with the care it deserves?

At Al Amanah International Academy, we believe every learner deserves patient, personalised guidance.

That's why we offer a FREE Tajweed Assessment reviewed by a qualified teacher.

There is no need to feel nervous.

This is not a judgment.

It is simply a starting point to understand where you are and how we can help you improve.

May Allah make our journey with His Book easy and beneficial. Ameen.
